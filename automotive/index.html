Automotive

A personal project I started for fun after an internship got me hooked on automotive and powersports work. It's unfinished, honestly more like a tenth of a full car than a complete build, but the suspension and drivetrain section is modeled and rigged. The exploded view breaks down every component, and the animation shows it actually functioning, suspension compressing, wheels turning, not just looking right from a still angle.
